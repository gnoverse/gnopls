# Security Policy

Do **not** open a public issue for a vulnerability.

Report it through
[GitHub Security Advisories on this repository](https://github.com/gnoverse/gnopls/security/advisories/new).

`gnopls` is a developer tool: it reads your source and runs on your machine, it is not part
of the gno.land chain. If what you found affects the chain, the VM or the standard
libraries, report it to [`gnolang/gno`](https://github.com/gnolang/gno/security/policy)
instead — that policy also covers email and PGP contact.
